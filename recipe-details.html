Chicken Curry

Category: Asian Difficulty: Medium

Description

A delicious and flavorful chicken curry made with aromatic spices and a rich sauce.

Ingredients

  • Chicken - 500 grams
  • Onion - 2 pieces
  • Tomato - 3 pieces
  • Garlic - 4 cloves
  • Ginger - 1 tablespoon
  • Cooking Oil - 2 tablespoons

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan.
  2. Add the onions and cook until golden brown.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, and spices.
  4. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  5. Add the tomatoes and cook until the sauce thickens.
  6. Cook until the chicken is fully done and serve hot.
